Built on three principles.

01

Generate from a topic or a system prompt.

Two modes for two kinds of work. Describe a topic and let the model expand it, or paste a system prompt and iterate from there.

02

Refine with structured feedback.

Tell the model what to change — tone, length, format, audience. Each refinement keeps a versioned history.

03

Keep everything in one library.

Personal saves stay private. Share with the community when you're ready. Browse what others have shipped.
