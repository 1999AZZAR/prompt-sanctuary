02 · The service

Prompt Sanctuary is a prompt engineering workbench. It helps you generate, refine, version, save, and share text prompts for large language models and image generation models. The service offers three main tools:

  • Generate — turn a topic or system prompt into a structured prompt.
  • Refine — improve an existing prompt using a quick action or custom instructions; every refinement is versioned.
  • Library — save, search, share, and revisit your prompts. Browse prompts shared by the community.

We may add, change, or remove features at any time. We will do our best to give reasonable notice for material changes.

06 · Credits and virtual currency

The service uses a virtual currency called credits. Credits are used to pay for generation and refinement operations. New accounts receive a starting balance.

  • Credits have no real-world monetary value and cannot be exchanged for cash.
  • Credits are not transferable between accounts.
  • Some credits expire after a period; the original signup balance does not.
  • If you add your own Google Generative AI API key, generations on your key do not consume credits; in some cases, providing a valid key awards additional credits as a thank-you.
